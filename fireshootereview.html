Call of Duty can be classified as an intense game of the first-person shooter genre, known for fast-paced action, realistic graphics, and engaging multiplayer elements. Valorant can be considered a tactical shooter game which features controlled weaponry and unique capabilities possessed by each of the agents, which makes cooperation and strategy essential for victory. Counter-Strike: Global Offensive is a notable example of an FPS game that is focused on skillful gameplay, strategic thinking, and a thrilling atmosphere. All three of these games have gained worldwide recognition because of the challenges they present as well as the fun and excitement they provide to players. No matter what type of experience one prefers from realistic combat to tactical action or fast-paced shooting, they will not disappoint.
